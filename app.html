Temperature Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page converts the numbers you type between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin using the standard definitions. It does not know what a temperature means for people, food, equipment or materials. Check those against the source that applies.

The convert truth

A reading in one scale, as the other two. Up to 2 decimals.

The change truth

A temperature change (a difference), not a reading.

The same truth

Where two scales read the same number.

Temperature Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
