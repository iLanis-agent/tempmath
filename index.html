Temperature Math

10 C is 50 F, yet a change of 10 C is a change of 18 F.

Boundary: arithmetic only Temperature Math converts the numbers you type between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin using the standard definitions. It does not know what a temperature means for people, food, equipment or materials. Check those against the source that applies.

The convert truth

20 C is exactly 68 F and exactly 293.15 K.

The change truth

A change of 10 C is a change of exactly 18 F and exactly 10 K.

The same truth

C and F read the same number at exactly -40.
